# Accessories — and what works with what

Source: `brand/catalog.txt` + technical training L9 & L11. Order refs shown where known.

## Guns (dispensing)
For 50 ml cartridges (fluid & pasty): **Manual Dispensing Gun** `AC-001`, **Pneumatic** `AC-001-P`.
For 400 ml XL cartridges: **Manual** `AC-021`, **Pneumatic** `AC-021-P`. A **battery** gun exists for
repetitive work. Malleables need **no gun**. Load: squeeze trigger to retract pistons, cartridge keyway
**down**, insert, ready.

## Injectors, nozzles & tips (mixing)
- **Standard injector** — screws onto the cartridge (¼-turn lock); internal vanes homogenise the two
  parts on extrusion. Handles **fluids and pasty** — ~99 % of jobs. **Single-use** (cured product blocks the vanes).
- **Tip / nozzle (embout)** — clips on the injector end for precise cavity work and fewer bubbles.
  **Fluids only** — *not for pasty* (too viscous, flow too low). **Reusable** if unclipped gently.
- **Needle injector** — 0.9 mm × 25 mm needle. **Fluids only** (mainly F20). For tiny bores.
- **Luer-Lock (LL) injector** — interchangeable needles **0.2–1 mm**, lengths **up to 20 cm**.
  **Fluids only** (mainly F20). For deep/very narrow passages. (Contact Plastiform for the needle range.)
- **Filins** — thin metal twists to chase a bubble stuck at the bottom of a tiny bore. Stir gently.

## Impression / cutting aids
- **Demolding rings** `AC-011` — 21 rings, 7 diameters. Add a ~1 cm grip of surplus product so a deep
  internal impression can be pulled out.
- **Double-Blade Cutter** — two parallel blades; standard **60 mm / 1 mm** slice. Also **100 / 200 mm**
  and **0.5–3 mm** custom (made in France by a cutler). Comes with a **cut guide** (prevents skewed cuts)
  and an **extraction card**. **Medium-hardness impressions only** — flexibles too soft, rigids too hard.
- **Plastin** — reusable technical modeling clay (non-greasy, never dries). Uses: flow **barrier**,
  through-hole **plug**, positioning **wedge**, and rolling over an impression to lift dust.

## Consumables
- **DN1 degreaser** — validated for Plastiform. Spray, let it evaporate (small nozzle for cavities).
  **Never acetone.** If DN1 is unavailable in your country, ask Plastiform for a validated alternative.
- **Dosing spoons** (malleable) — one spoon per component; **never cross them** (contamination).

## Kits
`KD-` kits (e.g. `KD-118` = 4× 400 ml F20 XL) · `ML-` **mallettes** — starter cases bundling cartridges,
the poster (range + max-constraint chart), cutter, plastin, spoons and rings.
