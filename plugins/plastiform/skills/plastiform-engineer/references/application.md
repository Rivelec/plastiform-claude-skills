# Application — step by step

Source: technical training L9. **Always start with the degrease.**

## 0. Degrease (mandatory, every time)
Spray **DN1** on the surface; use the small nozzle for cavities. It destroys oils/greases then
**evaporates on its own** — do not wipe (a cloth leaves fibres or redistributes grease). A greasy
surface won't cure: the impression stays tacky and measurements are false. **Never acetone.**

**Cure inhibitors** (datasheets, non-exhaustive): **latex** (incl. gloves), **sulfur compounds**,
**graphite**, oils/greases, **certain titanium alloys**. Contact with any of these can stop
polymerization. When in doubt about a material, **do a test drop on it first**.

## Pre-flight check (before each impression)
- [ ] Surface degreased with DN1, fully evaporated
- [ ] Extraction constraint calculated, product supports it
- [ ] Product chosen (right initial + final consistency)
- [ ] Correct injector mounted (± tip)
- [ ] Test drop done, colour uniform
- [ ] Demolding ring of the right diameter ready
- [ ] Extraction direction identified (lowest-constraint side)

## Fluid (F) — internal cavity
1. Cartridge keyway **down**; ¼-turn to lock the injector; clip the tip for cavity work.
2. **Colour test drop** on the side: output must be a single uniform colour (two colours = bad mix).
   This drop is also your cure witness.
3. **Inject from the bottom up**, tip **always immersed**, rubbing the walls in circles to chase bubbles.
4. After filling, add the **demolding ring** (~1 cm grip).
5. Cure: press a fingernail on the test drop — no mark = ready (Plastiform cures uniformly: if the top is
   hard, it all is). Colder = longer, warmer = shorter — but **never predict cure from temperature:
   the test drop IS the cure indicator.** The printed time assumes ~23 °C.
6. **Extract by pulling straight — never unscrew.**
7. Recap the cartridge (reusable to its printed date). Injector = single-use; tip = reusable.

## Pasty (P) — external surface
- **No tip** (too viscous). Keep the injector in contact with the surface, inject with light pressure so
  it infiltrates the detail. Lay a generous layer. Press flat with a card (the cutter's extraction card,
  a business card) to force it into fine detail and create a flat face for a clean cutter slice.

## Malleable (M) — by hand, no accessory
- Take **equal volumes** of base and catalyst with the **dedicated spoons** (one per component, never cross).
- Once mixed, you have **20–30 s**: knead **with fingertips only** (don't heat it) to a single uniform colour.
- Make a **smooth face** (the striated side is least faithful); apply by **compression**, full contact.
- Cure ~4–5 min. Reseal the pot (foil + cap).

## Double-blade cutter (medium hardness)
Interest face **up**, aligned in the **cut guide**; one clean pass (don't saw); lift the 2 mm slice with
the extraction card. A parallel 2 mm slice gives a flat profile for a profile projector or vision system.

## Through-holes
Choose the extraction direction where the constraint is **lowest** — the side with the **larger exit
diameter**. Identify it **before** injecting.
