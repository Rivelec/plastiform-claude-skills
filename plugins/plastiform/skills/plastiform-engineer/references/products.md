## Reading across the range (arguable, numbered facts)

- **Hardness span:** 04 → 90 Shore A **+ 51 Shore D** (≈ >100 ShA) — the whole elastomer scale.
- **Elongation span:** 5 % (P51 SD) → **300 % (P25)**.
- **Resolution:** 1 µm (F, P) · 10 µm (M) · demonstrated to **1 nm** on P80 Ra in optics.
- **Speed:** ~20 s (P51 SD) → 80 min work time (M80 TLp).
- **Bespoke formulation** is possible at sufficient volume (e.g. M80 TLp) — a catalogue competitor can't match this.

## Naming convention
`[letter F/P/M][Shore A value][optional suffix]`. Suffixes: **Ra** roughness · **LP** long-polymerisation ·
**SD** Shore D · **XL** large volume · **Visual** optical inspection · **Max** max elongation/constraint.
Exception: **P51 SD** is Shore **D**. Beware **F20 ≠ F20 XL** — not the same product with a different cure time.

## Packaging
Fluid/pasty → **50 ml cartridges** (keyed base). Malleable → **900 ml pots** (or 90 ml).
XL → **400 ml cartridge / 1.7 L bottle / 10.2 L drum**. Order refs are in `brand/catalog.txt` (`CA-`, `KD-`, `ML-`).
