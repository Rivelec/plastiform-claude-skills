# Storage, conservation & traceability

Source: training L15.

## Storage
Keep cartridges and pots in a **tempered** environment away from extremes, **low humidity** (no
condensation). Store cartridges **upright**, cap clean and closed. Recap immediately after use (avoid
contamination / parasitic set). For pots, respect the foil seal and the **dedicated spoons** per component.

## Shelf life
| Item | Duration | Condition |
|------|----------|-----------|
| Sealed cartridge | up to **3 years** | conforming storage, intact packaging |
| Opened cartridge | to the **printed date** | cap correctly refitted after use |
| Opened pot | to the **printed date** | foil + cap closed immediately |
| Cured impression | durable dimensional stability | protect from dust, intense UV, deformation |

## Traceability (minimum to record)
Part reference (drawing/lot/serial) · date & time of impression · **full product ref** (F20, P80 Ra…) ·
operator · measurement method & instrument · result (conform / non-conform + measured value).

## Archiving impressions
Keep the physical impression when it serves as a quality witness, an expertise support, or a comparison
reference during a non-conformity. Use identified bags/boxes (one per critical impression), avoid
mechanical compression, and link each impression to its traceability record. Define a retention period
per your customer/regulatory requirements.
