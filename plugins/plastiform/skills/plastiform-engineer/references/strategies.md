# Strategy — strong constraints & big parts

Source: training L10-L12. Reach here when the Finder returns no product, or the product it returns
can't be measured with your instrument.

## Strategy 1 — Partial impression (the default when you can)
Replicate only a cross-section instead of the full cavity. If you **don't need a diameter/radius** —
just a profile or surface state — do this even at normal constraint.

- Orient the part to expose the section of interest.
- Use **plastin** as a barrier to bound the zone and stop a fluid from running.
- Apply a **pasty or malleable** (P35, M70) for precise placement; compress lightly.
- Extraction constraint becomes ~0 → **medium and rigid products open up**, and you save product.

## Strategy 2 — Temporary void + reinjection (full impression, C too high)
When you must replicate the full form and C exceeds every product (typically **> 30 %**):

1. DN1, dry, mount injector, colour test drop.
2. **Inject partially** — fill the detail but leave a deliberate **central air pocket** (a straw/pen can hold the void).
3. Cure fully (fingernail test on the witness drop).
4. **Extract pulling straight** — the void lets the impression compress past the undercut without tearing.
5. **Reinject F20** into the void to rebuild the missing geometry.
6. Second cure; verify continuity and no bubbles on the useful zone before measuring.

Advanced — practise on a scrap part first; the first/second-injection interface can leave micro-offsets
measurable on tight tolerances.

## Extraction direction
Through-part with two openings → extract from the side with the **largest exit diameter** (lowest C).
Identify it before injecting.

## XL — big volumes (fluid only)
Switch to XL for **large dimensions (> 20 mm Ø), series production, or a longer work window**. Same
±1 µm precision; longer cure is an advantage on big/complex parts. Packaging: 400 ml cartridge / 1.7 L
bottle / 10.2 L drum; drum is hand-mixed. **F20 ≠ F20 XL.** Sizing: geometric volume **+ 20 %** margin.
Common: F20 XL / F30 XL (complex internal), **F50 XL** (series moulds/protection), F85 XL (rigid, no undercut).
